### Key Findings
- The Fabrication Monopoly: Over 90 percent of sub-5nm commercial chips are produced by a single company: TSMC in Taiwan.
- The HBM Chokepoint: High Bandwidth Memory from South Korea (SK Hynix and Samsung) is mandatory for AI accelerators to function without data bottlenecks.
- Advanced Packaging: Final performance gains increasingly rely on 3D chiplet packaging concentrated in Malaysia and Singapore.
- Capital & Time Barrier: A modern leading-edge fabrication plant costs upwards of 20 billion dollars and requires decades to build an integrated ecosystem.
