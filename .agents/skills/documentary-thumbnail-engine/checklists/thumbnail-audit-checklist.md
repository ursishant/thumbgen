# High-CTR Thumbnail & Packaging Quality Audit

Run every thumbnail and prompt directive through this rigorous 6-gate audit before exporting or finalizing.

---

## 1. Zero Word Repetition & Phrasing Purity (Anti-Duplication)
- [ ] **Single Instance Hook**: Does the core hook word (e.g. `THE CASINO`, `TRAPPED`) appear **strictly once** on the visual canvas?
- [ ] **No Twin Labels or Duplicate Badges**: Are there any redundant tape strips, dual stamps, or repeated badges displaying the same text twice?
- [ ] **Prompt Word Economy**: Is the generation prompt devoid of repetitive adjectives (e.g., no repeating "dark, dark", "gritty gritty", or stacked synonyms)?

---

## 2. The 1-Inch Mobile Preview Test (1.2-Second Rule)
- [ ] **Size Down**: Zoom out until the thumbnail is 1 inch wide (~160px width) on your monitor.
- [ ] **Instant Focal Point**: Is the main subject's face or central symbol instantly identifiable within 0.5 seconds?
- [ ] **Text Legibility**: Can the headline text be read effortlessly at thumbnail size on a mobile phone screen?

---

## 3. Visual Beauty & Aesthetic Elegance
- [ ] **Color Discipline**: Are there 3 or fewer dominant colors in the palette (e.g., Deep Charcoal `#121212`, Natural Ivory `#F4EFEA`, and Urgent Crimson `#C8102E`)?
- [ ] **Lighting Sophistication**: Does the image feature dramatic chiaroscuro with soft gradient roll-offs rather than flat, washed-out lighting?
- [ ] **Tactile Authenticity**: Does the composition celebrate physical materiality (paper fibers, newsprint halftone dots, canvas burlap, real tape, tattered edges) instead of plastic AI airbrushing?

---

## 4. The 3-Second Curiosity Gap & Click-Worthiness (CTR)
- [ ] **Narrative Tension**: Does the image ask an implicit question that the viewer cannot answer without watching?
- [ ] **Title Synergy**: Does the thumbnail text complement the video title rather than repeating it word-for-word?
- [ ] **Authentic Facial Gravitas**: Does the subject's expression provoke genuine human intrigue (pensive, stoic, hollowed-out, conflicted) instead of exaggerated YouTube caricature faces?

---

## 5. Composition & Depth Verification
- [ ] **3-Plane Stacking**: Does the composition have clear foreground, midground (hero), and background planes?
- [ ] **Typographic Anchoring**: Is the headline tucked behind the subject or anchored on physical tape/badge (not floating aimlessly)?
- [ ] **Clean Silhouettes**: Are the cutout edges crisp, free of green/fringing halos, and properly shadowed?

---

## 6. Safe Zones & Non-Intrusive Workflow
- [ ] **Timestamp Safe Zone**: Is critical text and visual information kept clear of the bottom-right corner (where YouTube overlays the video duration timestamp)?
- [ ] **Background Execution**: Were assets generated and extracted without hijacking the user's active screen, stealing window focus, or freezing their cursor?
