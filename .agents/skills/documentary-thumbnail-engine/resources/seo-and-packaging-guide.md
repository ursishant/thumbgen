# YouTube SEO, Packaging & Channel Growth Guide

A complete packaging architecture designed to help small and growing documentary channels maximize YouTube algorithmic distribution, CTR, and search discoverability.

---

## 1. The 3 High-CTR Title Formulas

Never write generic, dry academic titles. Small channels must balance **high search intent (SEO)** with **high psychological curiosity (CTR)**. Every video must offer 3 distinct title angles:

### Angle A: The Geopolitical / Systemic Crisis (High Stakes)
- **Formula**: `[Subject]'s [Crisis/Scandal]: Why [Immense Consequence]`
- **Examples**:
  - *NVIDIA's China Scandal: Why Washington is Terrified*
  - *The $108B Empire: How One Family Took Over India*
  - *Starlink's Undersea Limit: The Real Backbone of the Internet*

### Angle B: The Investigative Question / Revelation (Curiosity Gap)
- **Formula**: `The Hidden [System/Lie] Behind [Famous Company/Person]`
- **Examples**:
  - *The Secret Network Smuggling NVIDIA Chips Into China*
  - *The 2005 Trade Error That Made an Unknown Trader $100 Million*
  - *Who Really Controls the AI Chip Supply Chain?*

### Angle C: The Provocative Accusation / Ultimatum (Emotional Hook)
- **Formula**: `Did [Subject] Just Trigger the Next [Catastrophe]?`
- **Examples**:
  - *Did Jensen Huang Just Risk NVIDIA's Entire Future?*
  - *Why the AI Bubble is Facing Its Biggest Threat Yet*
  - *The Investigation That Could Break Big Tech's Most Powerful Monopoly*

## 3. Algorithm Keywords & Tag Architecture

Small channels need a balance of **broad reach tags** and **specific long-tail search tags**:

### 1. Primary High-Volume Target Keywords (Broad):
`NVIDIA`, `Artificial Intelligence`, `Jensen Huang`, `US China Tech War`, `AI Chips`

### 2. Long-Tail Search Intent Keywords (Rank-Building):
`NVIDIA China chip ban`, `Megaspeed NVIDIA investigation`, `H100 GPU export restrictions`, `how chips are smuggled to China`, `NVIDIA national security probe`, `AI bubble burst documentary`

### 3. Competitor & Topic Affinity Keywords:
`Vox style documentary`, `Johnny Harris investigative`, `Magnates Media business story`, `ColdFusion tech history`

### 4. Optimal Hashtag Selection (Top 3 for YouTube Header):
`#NVIDIA` `#AIWar` `#TechDocumentary`
