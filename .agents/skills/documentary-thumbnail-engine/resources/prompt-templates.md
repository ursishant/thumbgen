# Google Flow & Nano Banana Pro Prompting Recipes

These detailed prompt recipes are engineered specifically for **Google Flow** using **Nano Banana Pro**. They generate cinematic, documentary-grade base assets with tactile physical materiality, sharp subject characterization, and negative space tailored for Ishant's graphic overlays.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Anti-Repetition & Quality Directives**:
> - **Zero Duplicate Words**: Nano Banana Pro must always be explicitly instructed: `"Text '[HOOK]' must appear EXACTLY ONCE on the visual element. Strictly forbid duplicate words, repeating label tags, repeating badges, or double text stamps."`
> - **Concise Word Economy**: Prompts must avoid stacking redundant adjectives (e.g., avoid "dark, dark, moody, shadowy, gloomy" - use precise optical terminology instead).
> - **Beauty & CTR Polish**: Ensure high micro-contrast, tactile textures (burlap, newsprint, duct tape, paperclips), and authentic human expressions.

---

## Master Architecture for Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro

Every prompt fed to Google Flow / Nano Banana Pro should follow this structured blueprint:

```text
A cinematic 16:9 documentary editorial photograph of [SUBJECT & ATTIRE], captured with an intense, contemplative [EMOTION/EXPRESSION] looking [GAZE DIRECTION]. 
Lighting: Harsh directional chiaroscuro key lighting from the side with dramatic shadows, complemented by a sharp crimson-red [OR SPECIFIC COLOR] rim light defining the jawline and shoulders.
Environment & Materials: Set against a [BACKGROUND & TEXTURE DETAILS], featuring tactile [PHYSICAL PROPS: burlap weave / manila folder / twine string / newspaper clippings / slot machine reels].
Typography & Text Placement: Single headline text reading '[HOOK]' rendered in bold distressed condensed woodblock typography, anchored on [PHYSICAL MEDIUM: duct tape / red cloth patch / paper header].
Negative Constraints: The text '[HOOK]' appears EXACTLY ONCE. Strictly forbid duplicate words, repeating label tags, dual badges, or redundant text stamps.
Composition: Wide 16:9 cinematic framing with the subject positioned on the [LEFT / RIGHT / CENTER] third, leaving clean negative space for visual balance.
Style & Camera Specs: Shot on Hasselblad H6D-100c with a 50mm f/2.8 lens. High micro-contrast, crisp skin pores, natural film grain, authentic paper texture bleed. Documentary realism, non-CGI, raw authentic editorial aesthetic.
```
